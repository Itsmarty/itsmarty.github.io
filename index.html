Flagship transformation · PayPal

From publishing information to anticipating and resolving customer needs.

A multi-era platform transformation that shows the method in action—from governed enterprise content to semantic intelligence, connected operations, real-time journeys, and agentic resolution.

Content became knowledge. Knowledge became intelligence. Intelligence became proactive action.

The story is not a single product. It is the compounding evolution of platforms, data, teams, and operating models—each generation preserving what worked, expanding reuse, and creating the foundation for the next.
